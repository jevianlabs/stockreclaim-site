The weekly digest is a short email that keeps your returns number in front of you
without opening the app. It's available on Starter and Growth.

## What it contains

- New exceptions since the last digest, and what they're worth.
- Your running **recovery ledger** — dollars flagged and dollars recovered since install.
- A single line you can act on, e.g. *"3 new exceptions, $210 not restocked."*

## Turning it on

In **Settings → Weekly digest**, switch it on and set:

- the **recipient** address,
- the **day** of the week to send.

The email goes out on that weekday, in your store's local morning.

## A clean week still sends

If nothing new turned up, the digest still arrives — *"No exceptions this week.
$12,480 checked."* Silence would be indistinguishable from a broken integration,
so a clean week is reported as the result it is.

## Why it exists

An audit answers a question once; a subscription earns its keep only if the
question keeps getting answered. The digest is what turns a one-time diagnosis
into an ongoing check — especially as staff change and new habits drift.
