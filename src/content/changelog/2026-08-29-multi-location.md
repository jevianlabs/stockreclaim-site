---

On Growth, the audit is now **location-aware end to end**. Every rule carries a
location, exceptions show and filter by it, and CSV exports include it.

You can choose which locations to include in **Settings**, and the digest and
reconciliation packet honor that scope. Refunds and open returns resolve to the
order's fulfillment location automatically.
