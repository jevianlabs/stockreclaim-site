Two ways to keep the number in front of you, on Starter and Growth:

- **The weekly digest** — a short Monday-morning email with new exceptions and
  what they're worth. A clean week still sends, so silence never looks like a
  broken app.
- **The recovery ledger** — dollars flagged and dollars recovered since install,
  on the dashboard and in every digest. It's a running score, and it only goes
  up while you're subscribed.
