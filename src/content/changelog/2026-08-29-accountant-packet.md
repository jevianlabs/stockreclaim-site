Growth now produces a **monthly reconciliation packet** — a month-end PDF with
totals recovered, written off and outstanding, a by-cause breakdown, and the
decision-by-decision detail with write-off notes. Download it any time from the
**Decisions** page.

With the **accountant seat**, that packet is emailed to a second recipient on the
day of the month you choose — no forwarding, and one more person who depends on
the numbers.
