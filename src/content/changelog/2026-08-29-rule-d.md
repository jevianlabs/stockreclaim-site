Growth plans now include **Rule D**, the check no returns portal runs on itself.

When a restock is *recorded* — a refund with Restock checked, or a return
dispositioned as restocked — StockReclaim now watches whether the on-hand level
actually moved. If it didn't, that's a Rule D exception, merged into your
dashboard and digest alongside the others.

Rule D works from two sources: refunds and formal returns, with cross-source
deduplication so one physical restock is counted once. It's **forward-only** by
design — coverage grows the longer it runs.
