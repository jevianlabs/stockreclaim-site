Plans, trials and billing

The three plans, the 14-day trial, and how to upgrade or downgrade yourself without contacting support.

The plans

PlanPriceFor
Free$0Everyone at install. The full dollar figure and your top exceptions.
Starter$39/moThe complete audit: every exception, 12-month history, weekly digest, CSV, recovery ledger.
Growth$89/moContinuous assurance: Rule D monitoring, multi-location, and the monthly reconciliation packet.

See the full comparison on the pricing page.

The trial

Both paid plans include a 14-day free trial. Nothing is charged during the trial. Shopify tracks trial days over a rolling window, so uninstalling and reinstalling won’t start a fresh one.

No per-return fees

Every plan is a flat monthly price. StockReclaim never charges by the number of returns or refunds you process.

Upgrading and downgrading

You can change plans yourself, any time, from Settings → Manage plan inside the app. That opens Shopify’s own billing screen — you never need to email us to change or cancel a plan.

How billing is handled

All charges run through Shopify App Pricing. StockReclaim never sees or stores your card. Shopify handles the charge, proration and the trial clock.

The headline is never gated

Whatever plan you’re on — including Free — you always see the headline dollar figure and the split by cause. Plans change what you can do about it and how far back you can look, never whether you can see the total.
